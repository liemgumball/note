# 📺 YouTube Channel Plan: Motivational & Focus AI Channel

## 🎯 Channel Goal
Create a faceless YouTube channel using AI tools to produce:  
- **Short motivational clips** for quick viral growth (YouTube Shorts).  
- **Focus/study music videos** (long-form for high watch time).  

Target audience:  
- Students, professionals, and anyone looking for motivation or productivity aids.  

Monetization timeline: **3-6 months**  
1. Hit 1,000 subs & 4,000 watch hours → Apply for AdSense.  
2. Add affiliate links (books, courses, productivity tools).  
3. Expand to digital products (motivational wallpapers, planners).

## 🏗️ Workflow: Make a Video in 1 Hour

1. **Script (10 min)**  
   - Use ChatGPT prompt:  
     > “Write a 60-second motivational script about [topic] in an inspiring tone.”  

2. **Voiceover (10 min)**  
   - Generate in **ElevenLabs** → Download.  

3. **Visuals (20 min)**  
   - Use **RunwayML** to generate scenes or combine stock footage.  

4. **Edit (15 min)**  
   - Add background music, captions, transitions (CapCut).  

5. **Upload (5 min)**  
   - SEO-friendly title, description, tags.  
   - Design thumbnail with **Canva**.

## 📈 Growth Strategy

✅ Start with **2-3 videos/week** (mix Shorts & long videos).  
✅ Focus on **viral Shorts** to grow subs quickly.  
✅ Post at the **same time every week** for algorithm consistency.  
✅ Share on **Reddit, TikTok, Instagram Reels** to pull extra traffic.
