# 🏋️‍♂️ 3-Month Gym Plan (Morning Workout 7–8 AM)

## Month 1 (Adaptation & Technique)
- **Sessions:** 3 per week (with rest days in between)  
- **Goal:** learn proper form, avoid excessive soreness  

### Day 1 – Upper (Chest – Shoulders – Arms)
- Bench Press – 3×8–10  
- Incline Dumbbell Press – 3×10  
- Overhead Press – 3×10  
- Lateral Raise – 3×12–15  
- Biceps Curl – 2×12  
- Triceps Pushdown – 2×12  

### Day 2 – Lower (Legs – Glutes – Core)
- Squat – 3×8–10  
- Romanian Deadlift – 3×8–10  
- Leg Press – 3×10–12  
- Lunge – 3×10 (each leg)  
- Standing Calf Raise – 3×12–15  
- Plank – 3×30–60s  

### Day 3 – Full Body (Light)
- Deadlift – 3×6  
- Bench Press – 3×8  
- Pull-ups/Lat Pulldown – 3×8–10  
- Overhead Press – 2×10  
- Hanging Leg Raise – 2×12  

---

## Month 2 (Progressive Overload)
- **Sessions:** 4 per week (Upper – Lower – Rest – Upper – Lower)  
- **Goal:** gradually increase training volume and load  

### Schedule
- **Monday (7–8 AM):** Upper (Chest – Shoulders – Arms)  
- **Tuesday (7–8 AM):** Lower (Legs – Core)  
- **Thursday (7–8 AM):** Upper (Back – Rear Delts – Arms)  
- **Friday (7–8 AM):** Lower (Legs – Core)  

- Add 1 extra set to most exercises (4 sets instead of 3)  
- Increase weight by 2–5% weekly or add 1–2 reps  
- Focus on compound lifts (Squat, Deadlift, Bench, Row, Overhead Press)  

---

## Month 3 (Stability & Growth)
- **Sessions:** 4–5 per week (depending on recovery)  
- **Goal:** maintain form, push heavier weights  

### Schedule
- **Monday (7–8 AM):** Chest – Shoulders – Arms  
- **Tuesday (7–8 AM):** Legs – Core  
- **Thursday (7–8 AM):** Back – Rear Delts – Triceps  
- **Friday (7–8 AM):** Legs – Core  
- **(Optional Saturday):** Light cardio + weak point training  

---

# 🍽️ Nutrition for Muscle & Weight Gain

## Guidelines
- **Caloric surplus:** +300–500 kcal/day  
- **Protein:** 1.6–2 g/kg (≈110–130 g/day for you)  
- **Carbs:** 4–6 g/kg (≈260–400 g/day) → rice, noodles, potatoes, bread  
- **Fat:** 0.8–1 g/kg (≈50–65 g/day) → eggs, nuts, fish, healthy oils  
- **Water:** 2–3 L/day  

---

## ⏰ Eating Schedule (Morning Training 7–8 AM)

### 6:15 AM – Pre-workout snack
- 1 banana or 1 slice of bread + peanut butter  
- ½ scoop whey protein (with water)  
- 300 ml water  

### 7:00–8:00 AM – Training

### 8:05 AM – Post-workout
- 1 scoop whey protein  
- 1 banana or a slice of bread  

### 8:30–8:45 AM – Breakfast (Main meal)
- 1–2 bowls of rice OR noodles/pho/vermicelli  
- 150–200 g chicken breast/fish/beef/shrimp  
- Mixed vegetables (morning glory, bok choy, broccoli, tomato)  
- Soup (pumpkin, gourd, vegetable with bone broth)  

### 10:30–11:00 AM – Snack
- 1 fruit (mango, papaya, watermelon)  
- 1 yogurt or soy milk  

### 12:30 PM – Lunch
- 2 bowls of rice  
- 150–200 g chicken/fish/beef  
- Stir-fried or boiled vegetables  
- Soup (tofu with tomato, vegetables with minced pork)  

### 3:30 PM – Afternoon snack
- Sweet potato or corn  
- 1 boiled egg  
- Optional: ½ scoop whey if protein intake is low  

### 6:30 PM – Dinner
- 1–2 bowls of rice  
- 150–200 g fish, chicken, or beef  
- Vegetables + soup  
- Tofu or eggs  

### 9:30 PM – Pre-bed
- 1 glass of milk or 1 yogurt  
- 20–30 g nuts (almonds, walnuts, peanuts)  

---

# 💡 Extra Tips
- Stick to familiar Vietnamese foods: rice, noodles, pho, potatoes  
- Animal protein: chicken, fish, beef, pork, eggs, seafood  
- Plant protein: tofu, soy milk, beans  
- Whey protein: **2 scoops/day** (1 post-workout, 1 as extra when needed)  
- Avoid excessive fried food and sugary drinks
